Black-and-white film footage, heavy grain. Locked low camera with a slight shake. The huge dome of water far ahead swells and bursts open: a colossal black armoured titan rises straight up out of the flooded plain, jagged horns first, then its helmeted head and massive shoulders, thick sheets of water pouring off its armour and crashing down. Rain falls hard. The knight in the foreground stands still, cape moving in the wind. Slow, heavy, enormous motion.
