# The Army That Lost to Birds

**Title:** The Great Emu War of 1932 🐦🪖

**Description:** In 1932 Australia sent soldiers with machine guns against an invasion of about 20,000 emus. The guns jammed, the trucks couldn't keep up, and by the army's own count it took ten bullets per bird. The emus never signed a peace treaty.

**Sources**
- Drought-driven emu migration into the wheatbelt of Western Australia (Campion district); many farmers were WWI veterans: Britannica "Emu War"; Wikipedia "Emu War".
- About 20,000 emus: Australian Geographic (2016) "Australia's emu wars"; History Hit "The Great Emu War".
- Major G.P.W. Meredith (Royal Australian Artillery), two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds; 2 Nov - 10 Dec 1932 in two phases: Wikipedia; Mental Floss.
- An early ambush at a dam ended when the gun jammed; a truck-mounted gun couldn't keep up over rough ground: Wikipedia; MeatEater.
- Emus sprint at about 48-50 km/h: Victoria Wildlife emu fact sheet.
- 9,860 rounds for 986 birds — the army's own (Meredith's) count, not independent: Wikipedia; Australian Geographic. ≈ 10 rounds per bird.
- Afterwards farmers went back to a bounty system, which proved more effective: Wikipedia.
- No birds are shown being hit (dust, feathers, jams and misses only). Meredith's "invulnerability of tanks" quote omitted (wording unverified).
