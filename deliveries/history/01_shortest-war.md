# The Shortest War in History

**Title:** The War That Lasted 38 Minutes ⏱️

**Description:** In August 1896, Britain gave Zanzibar's new sultan until 9 a.m. to leave the palace. The war that followed lasted about 38 minutes — and the losing side was sent the bill for the shells.

**Hashtags:** #history #shorts #historyfacts #zanzibar #war

**Sources**
- Sultan Hamad bin Thuwaini died 25 Aug 1896; Khalid bin Barghash took the throne without British approval; the ultimatum expired 09:00 on 27 Aug 1896: Britannica "Anglo-Zanzibar War" (britannica.com/event/Anglo-Zanzibar-War); Wikipedia "Anglo-Zanzibar War".
- Duration ~38 minutes (09:02-09:40 usually cited; scholars give 38-45 min; Britannica: no more than 45 min) — the shortest war on record: Atlas Obscura "The World's Shortest War"; Guinness World Records.
- The Zanzibari navy's royal yacht HHS Glasgow was sunk; its crew was rescued: Wikipedia; Mental Floss.
- Khalid fled to the German consulate; Hamoud bin Mohammed was installed as sultan the same day: Forces News "Was this the shortest war ever?".
- About 500 Zanzibari killed or wounded (many in the palace fire): Wikipedia; Forces News. Said once, briefly ("hundreds were hurt").
- Britain demanded payment for the shells fired; sources differ on who exactly was billed, so the script says "the losing side": Wikipedia; The Collector.
- The German Empire's 1896 flag was the black-white-red tricolour (no emblem shown).
