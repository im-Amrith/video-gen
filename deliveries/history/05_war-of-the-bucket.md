# The War of the Bucket

**Title:** The War Where the Winners Took Home a Bucket 🪣⚔️

**Description:** In 1325, Modena beat a much bigger Bolognese army at Zappolino — and chased them all the way home to grab a trophy: the wooden bucket from a well by the city gate. People still say the war started over that bucket. It didn't. It's still in Modena's town hall.

**Sources**
- Battle of Zappolino, 15 Nov 1325: Modena (Ghibelline) defeated Bologna (Guelph): Wikipedia "Battle of Zappolino"; Wikipedia "War of the Bucket".
- The real trigger: Modena seized Bologna's castle of Monteveglio; the "stolen bucket started the war" story is a myth: Wikipedia "War of the Bucket"; The Collector "The War of the Bucket".
- Armies ~32,000 Bolognese vs ~7,000 Modenese (said "about thirty thousand" / "about seven thousand"); Passerino Bonacolsi used a feigned river crossing: Wikipedia; HistoryNet "What we learned from the Battle of Zappolino".
- The Bolognese broke and fled before sunset; the Modenese chased them to Bologna's walls and took a wooden bucket from a well outside Porta San Felice as a trophy: Wikipedia; Bologna Welcome "Porta San Felice".
- The original bucket is in the Camerino dei Confirmati of Modena's Palazzo Comunale (town hall), a copy in the Ghirlandina tower: Comune di Modena; Visit Modena.
- Modena and Bologna are ~40 km apart (standard distance). Casualties not shown or quantified.
