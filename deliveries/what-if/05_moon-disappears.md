# What If the Moon Disappeared?

**Title:** What If the Moon Disappeared? 🌙❓

**Description:** Lose the Moon and the first thing you'd notice is the sea: tides shrink to about a third. Nights get darker, corals lose their calendar — and over millions of years Earth's tilt could swing wildly, sending the seasons bonkers.

**Hashtags:** #whatif #moon #space #science #shorts

**Sources**
- The Moon is the main driver of tides; the Sun's tidal pull is about half the Moon's (Science Norway, K. Aksnes; NOAA "What causes tides?"). Sun-only tides ≈ 0.46 / 1.46 ≈ one third of today's.
- Without the Moon, tides would be roughly a third as big (starlust.org; Live Science "What would happen if the moon disappeared tomorrow?").
- Coral mass spawning on the Great Barrier Reef is cued by moonlight (cry2 gene most active on full-moon nights): University of Queensland news 2007-10-18; Kaniewska et al., eLife 4:e09991 (2015).
- Earth's tilt today varies ~22.1°-24.5° on a ~41,000-year cycle (Wikipedia "Axial tilt"; arXiv:1409.2881).
- Without the Moon the tilt could vary chaotically from nearly 0° to ~85°: Laskar, Joutel & Robutel, Nature 361, 615-617 (1993).
- Such changes would take millions of years; seasons stay normal for a very long time (Science Norway; Live Science).
- The Moon has been with Earth for ~4.5 billion years (NASA Moon facts) → "billions of years".
