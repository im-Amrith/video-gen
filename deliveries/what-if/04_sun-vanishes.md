# What If the Sun Vanished?

**Title:** What If the Sun Vanished? ☀️❌

**Description:** If the Sun disappeared right now, you wouldn't know for 8 minutes and 20 seconds. Then the lights go out, Earth flies off in a straight line, and the oceans freeze over — but deep down by the vents, the shrimp just carry on.

**Hashtags:** #whatif #space #sun #science #shorts

**Sources**
- Sunlight takes ~8 min 20 s to reach Earth; for that long nobody would notice (NRAO public pages; Live Science "What would happen to Earth if the sun suddenly vanished?"; Neil deGrasse Tyson quoted there).
- Gravity changes travel at the speed of light: GW170817 gravitational waves and GRB 170817A gamma rays arrived 1.7 s apart after ~130 million years; speed difference < ~1e-15 (LIGO science summary "GW170817GRB"; arXiv:1710.05805).
- Moonlight is reflected sunlight, so the Moon goes dark too (standard astronomy).
- With no Sun to orbit, Earth continues in a straight line at its orbital speed, ~30 km/s (Live Science; MIT "If the Sun disappeared" Q&A).
- The Moon stays bound to Earth and keeps orbiting it (Earth-Moon system is gravitationally bound; standard).
- Cooling ~20 °C per day at first, below freezing within ~a week (estimates shared with Live Science; Vsauce summary) — popular-science estimates.
- Oceans freeze from the top; the ice insulates the water below, which stays liquid for many thousands of years (Live Science; Vsauce; futura-sciences).
- Hydrothermal-vent ecosystems (tube worms, shrimp, microbes) run on chemosynthesis, not sunlight (NOAA Ocean Exploration; WHOI).
