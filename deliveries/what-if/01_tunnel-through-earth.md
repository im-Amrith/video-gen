# What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth?

**Title:** What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth? 🕳️🌍

**Description:** Jump into a tunnel drilled from the North Pole to the South Pole and you'd fall through the entire planet in about 38 minutes, faster than 28,000 km/h, past liquid iron hotter than the Sun's surface. But leave the air in, and you'd end up stuck floating at the center of the Earth.
