# The Shortest War in History

**Title:** The War That Lasted 38 Minutes ⏱️

**Description:** In August 1896, Britain gave Zanzibar's new sultan until 9 a.m. to leave the palace. The war that followed lasted about 38 minutes — and the losing side was sent the bill for the shells.
