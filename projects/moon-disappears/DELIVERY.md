# What If the Moon Disappeared?

**Title:** What If the Moon Disappeared? 🌙❓

**Description:** Lose the Moon and the first thing you'd notice is the sea: tides shrink to about a third. Nights get darker, corals lose their calendar — and over millions of years Earth's tilt could swing wildly, sending the seasons bonkers.
