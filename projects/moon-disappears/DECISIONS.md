# Decisions — moon-disappears (What If ep. 5)
- Hook: the Moon pops out of the night sky over the beach and the tide slides away (flash + kick).
- Tides shown as a translucent bulge on the stylised globe, stretched toward the Moon; after the pop it shrinks to a small Sun bulge.
- Tilt: a red axis rod through the globe; with the Moon it wobbles ±1.2° around 23.3°, without it it swings toward ~85° — Pip clings to the top.
- "Seasons go bonkers": the shot flips every 0.4 s between snow and a blazing sun pictogram, calendar pages flying.
- The full-moon lux figure could not be sourced, so the script says only "much darker".
