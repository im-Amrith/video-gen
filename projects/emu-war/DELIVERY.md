# The Army That Lost to Birds

**Title:** The Great Emu War of 1932 🐦🪖

**Description:** In 1932 Australia sent soldiers with machine guns against an invasion of about 20,000 emus. The guns jammed, the trucks couldn't keep up, and by the army's own count it took ten bullets per bird. The emus never signed a peace treaty.
