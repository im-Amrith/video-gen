# Decisions — earth-stops-spinning (What If ep. 2)
- Built on the shared toon engine + kit (src/videos/toon/kit.ts: Pip, pictograms, stylised globe shader, sea).
- Hook: Pip riding a tiny spinning planet; the stop lands on "stopped?" with a flash + kick.
- The globe is a stylised shader (noise continents, no real map/borders, per DIRECTOR §13 "no borders"); uOcean slides the
  water to the poles, uBulge squashes the poles. The supercontinent shot is a wide orbit (upright cards can't stand on the equator).
- Day/night year: calendar pictograms (red = day half, blue = night half) instead of text; the alarm clock gag.
- Moon brake: over-the-Moon shot with Pip sitting on it, faint tidal bulge facing the Moon.
- Portrait framing rule learned in ep. 1: a 12 m globe needs the camera ~30 m out.
