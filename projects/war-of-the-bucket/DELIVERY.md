# The War of the Bucket

**Title:** The War Where the Winners Took Home a Bucket 🪣⚔️

**Description:** In 1325, Modena beat a much bigger Bolognese army at Zappolino — and chased them all the way home to grab a trophy: the wooden bucket from a well by the city gate. People still say the war started over that bucket. It didn't. It's still in Modena's town hall.
