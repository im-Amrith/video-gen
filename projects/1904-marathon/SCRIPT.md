# SCRIPT — 1904-marathon (history ep. 4, 3D cartoon)

## Sources
- 30 Aug 1904, St. Louis; ~32 °C (90 °F); dusty unpaved roads; 32 starters, 14 finishers: Wikipedia "Athletics at the 1904 Summer Olympics – Men's marathon"; Olympics.com "St. Louis marathon: the strangest race in Olympic history".
- One water station (most accounts: ~12 miles), linked to organiser James Sullivan's interest in "purposeful dehydration": Olympics.com; Running Magazine.
- Fred Lorz stopped after ~9 miles, rode in a car (~11 miles) until it broke down, jogged to the finish, was cheered as the winner, said it was a joke; banned (later reduced); won the 1905 Boston Marathon: Wikipedia "Frederick Lorz"; Jalopnik.
- Thomas Hicks was given strychnine, egg whites and brandy by his trainers; finished hallucinating and helped over the line in ~3:28 (≈ three and a half hours): Smithsonian Magazine; IOC "The strange case of the St Louis 1904 marathon"; ChemistryViews.
- Andarín Carvajal (Cuban mail carrier) hadn't eaten for ~40 hours, ate bad (rotten or unripe) apples from an orchard, got cramps, napped, and still finished 4th: Wikipedia "Andarín Carvajal"; Britannica.
- Framed as "a terrible 1904 idea" — not advice. The dust injury of William Garcia is omitted; Len Taunyane dog story omitted (unverified).
