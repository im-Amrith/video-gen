# What If the Sun Vanished?

**Hook:** "If the Sun vanished right now, you wouldn't know for eight minutes and twenty seconds."

**Story in 5 beats:** hook (8 min 20 s of not knowing) → setting (light and gravity both travel at light speed) →
escalation (the dark arrives and Earth leaves its orbit in a straight line) → payoff (the surface cools ~20 °C a day at first; oceans freeze over) →
twist (the ice lid insulates the deep ocean, and life around volcanic vents could carry on).

**Key facts + sources:**
- Sunlight takes ~8 min 20 s (1 AU ÷ c). NASA; NRAO public pages.
- Changes in gravity propagate at the speed of light (confirmed by GW170817). LIGO.
- Earth flies off on a tangent at ~30 km/s. Live Science "What would happen to Earth if the sun suddenly vanished?"; N. deGrasse Tyson quote.
- Cooling ~20 °C per day at first (Live Science, quoting Summers).
- Floating ice insulates water below; hydrothermal vent ecosystems don't need sunlight. NOAA Ocean Exploration.

**Visual ideas:** a countdown clock as an iso block; the orbit drawn as a route line that snaps straight; a thermometer pin dropping;
a cutaway of an ocean with an ice lid and a glowing vent at the bottom.
