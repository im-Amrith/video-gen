# What If the Moon Disappeared?

**Hook:** "Tonight the Moon is gone. The first thing you'd notice isn't the sky. It's the sea."

**Story in 5 beats:** hook (no Moon) → setting (the Moon raises most of our tides) → escalation (tides shrink to about a third,
nights get much darker) → payoff (without the Moon's steadying pull, Earth's tilt could wander, and seasons go wild) →
twist (it wouldn't happen fast: seasons stay normal for a very long time).

**Key facts + sources:**
- The Sun's tidal pull is about half the Moon's; without the Moon tides would be roughly a third as big. Science Norway (Aksnes); NOAA "What causes tides?".
- Today's tilt stays within ~22-24.5°; without the Moon it could drift chaotically over millions of years (Laskar et al., *Nature* 1993). Live Science "What would happen if the moon disappeared tomorrow?".
- Changes to the tilt would take a very long time. Science Norway; Live Science.
- Moonlight: full Moon ~0.1-0.3 lux. (Verify a source before the script.)
