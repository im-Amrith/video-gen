# What If the Earth Stopped Spinning?

**Hook:** "Right now you're moving at sixteen hundred kilometres an hour. Let's hit the brakes."

**Story in 5 beats:** hook (the equator moves ~1,670 km/h) → setting (spin makes Earth bulge ~21 km at the equator) →
escalation (slowly stop it: the bulge relaxes, oceans slide toward the poles) → payoff (one giant equatorial continent, two polar oceans) →
twist (a single day lasts a whole year: six months of daylight, six months of night).

**Key facts + sources:**
- Equatorial speed ≈ 40,075 km ÷ 23.93 h ≈ 1,670 km/h. Circumference: NASA Earth fact sheet.
- Equatorial radius 6,378 km vs polar 6,357 km (≈21 km bulge). NASA Earth fact sheet.
- Oceans migrate to the poles, an equatorial megacontinent emerges; most of Europe and Russia under the northern ocean. Witold Fraczek, Esri ArcUser 2010, "If the Earth Stood Still" (esri.com/news/arcuser/0610/nospin.html).
- Without rotation, a solar day = one orbit (~365 days). Basic orbital mechanics.
- A sudden stop is not physically possible (angular momentum); we show the slow stop. IFLScience; Popular Science 2010.

**Visual ideas:** an iso slice of the planet with a water layer that slides poleward; a tiny town that ends up on a new coast; a sun dial
whose shadow takes a year to turn; Isotype day/night calendar.
