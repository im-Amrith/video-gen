# The Worst Olympic Marathon Ever Run

**Hook (first line of the voice-over):** "The man who crossed the finish line first at the 1904 Olympic marathon had spent eleven miles of it in a car."

**Story in 5 beats:** hook (a runner waving from the back seat of an old car) → setting (St. Louis, 30 Aug 1904: about 32 °C, dusty dirt roads, cars and horses kicking up dust, and only one water station, near mile 12; 32 starters) → escalation (Fred Lorz quits after ~9 miles, rides in a car until it breaks down, then jogs to the line and is cheered as the winner; Thomas Hicks is kept going by his trainers with strychnine, raw egg and brandy; Cuban mail carrier Andarín Carvajal, who hasn't eaten in ~40 hours, eats apples from an orchard, gets cramps, naps by the road) → snap (Lorz is caught, says it was a joke; Hicks is half-carried over the line in about 3 h 28 min, hallucinating; only 14 finish; Carvajal, after his nap, still comes 4th) → deadpan + twist ("Luckily, the real winner got his gold. Unluckily, the man who took a car won Boston the next year. On foot.")

**Key facts + sources:**
- 32 starters from several nations, 14 finishers; heat about 32 °C (90 °F); dusty unpaved course. Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1904_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_marathon ; Olympics.com, https://olympics.com/en/news/st-louis-marathon-the-strangest-race-in-olympic-history
- One water station (most accounts put it at ~12 miles), linked to organiser James Sullivan's interest in "purposeful dehydration". Olympics.com (above); Running Magazine, https://runningmagazine.ca/?p=68422
- Lorz stopped after ~9 miles, rode in a car (about 11 miles) until it broke down, finished first, said it was a joke; AAU life ban later cut to six months; he won Boston 1905. Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Lorz ; Jalopnik, https://jalopnik.com/5928842/the-first-winner-of-the-1904-olympic-marathon-used-a-car-the-second-winner-used-drugs-and-booze
- Hicks was given strychnine, egg whites and brandy by his trainers; he finished hallucinating and was helped over the line; winning time ~3:28 (sources: 3:28:45 / 3:28:53). Smithsonian, https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/how-the-1904-marathon-became-one-of-the-weirdest-olympic-events-of-all-time-14910747/ ; IOC, https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/the-strange-case-of-the-st-louis-1904-marathon ; ChemistryViews, https://www.chemistryviews.org/?p=34968
- Carvajal had not eaten for ~40 hours, ate apples from an orchard (accounts say rotten or unripe), got cramps, napped, and still finished 4th. Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andar%C3%ADn_Carvajal ; Britannica, https://www.britannica.com/topic/Felix-Carvajal-How-to-Make-Friends-at-a-Marathon-1795606

**Visual ideas:**
- Frame 0: Lorz lounging in the passenger seat of a boxy 1904 car, waving at runners eating his dust.
- A dirt road where every passing car/horse leaves a giant cartoon dust cloud; runners emerge coughing, grey from head to toe.
- One lonely water well with a sign "WATER: 1"; a line of runners stretching to the horizon.
- Hicks's trainers as a pit crew with a tiny bottle, an egg and a brandy flask; Hicks's eyes spin, the road wobbles.
- Carvajal in long trousers cut into shorts, under an apple tree; he takes a bite, his face goes green, he lies down with a "Zzz" bubble.
- Finish: two trainers carry Hicks whose legs still pedal in the air; a medal glints.
