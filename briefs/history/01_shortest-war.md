# The War That Ended Before Breakfast Was Cold

**Hook (first line of the voice-over):** "The shortest war in history lasted about thirty-eight minutes. You've had longer phone calls."

**Story in 5 beats:** hook (a pocket watch ticking over a palace) → setting (Zanzibar, 25 Aug 1896: the pro-British sultan Hamad dies; his cousin Khalid bin Barghash moves into the palace without Britain's OK) → escalation (Britain sends warships and an ultimatum: leave by 9:00 a.m. on 27 Aug; Khalid barricades the palace and refuses) → snap (9:00 comes, the ships open fire; the palace's wooden bits catch fire, the sultan's one-ship navy, the royal yacht *Glasgow*, is sunk; by about 9:40 the guns stop and Khalid has slipped out to the German consulate) → deadpan + twist ("Luckily, it was over in under an hour. Unluckily, the losers were sent the bill for the shells.")

**Key facts + sources:**
- Hamad bin Thuwaini died 25 Aug 1896; Khalid bin Barghash took the throne without British approval; ultimatum expired 09:00 on 27 Aug. Britannica, https://www.britannica.com/event/Anglo-Zanzibar-War ; Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Zanzibar_War
- Duration: usually 09:02-09:40 (≈38 min); official records don't fix it exactly, scholars cite 38-45 min; Britannica says no more than 45 min. Atlas Obscura, https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/worlds-shortest-war ; Britannica (above)
- The royal yacht HHS *Glasgow* (the Zanzibari "navy") was sunk; its crew was rescued. Wikipedia (above); Mental Floss, https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/49030/worlds-shortest-war
- Khalid fled to the German consulate; Britain installed Hamoud bin Mohammed as sultan the same day. Forces News, https://www.forcesnews.com/news/was-shortest-ever-war-military-history
- Britain demanded repayment for the shells fired; sources differ on whether the Zanzibar government or Khalid's supporters were billed. Wikipedia (above); The Collector, https://www.thecollector.com/anglo-zanzibar-war/
- Casualties: about 500 Zanzibari killed or wounded (many in the palace fire); one British sailor wounded. Wikipedia (above); Forces News (above)

**Visual ideas:**
- Giant pocket watch floating in the harbour sky, its minute hand as the countdown to 9:00; it ticks loudly on every cut.
- A palace of painted wooden balconies on a turquoise bay; three grey cartoon warships glide in and line up like ducks.
- The royal yacht: a fancy white boat with one bean-head sailor saluting; it settles gently into the water (no gore), sailor bobs away in a rowing boat.
- Khalid sneaking out a side door with a suitcase toward a little consulate with a German flag (simple tricolour, no emblems).
- Payoff gag: a paper invoice slides under the palace door: "SHELLS: 1 x war".

**Notes:** Light and deadpan. Real people died in the palace fire: say "hundreds were hurt" once, briefly, and never show injury; keep the comedy on the clock and the bill, not on the victims. Don't name the British ships' guns or show explosions hitting people; show flags, smoke and splashes. Say "about thirty-eight minutes" (never "exactly"). Dropped: the claim that the ships were HMS *St George*/*Glasgow* sinking each other (I could not verify specific British ship roles); the exact shell counts (500 shells, 4,100 MG rounds) come from a blog only, so leave them out. Hold the bill line soft: "the losing side got the bill".
