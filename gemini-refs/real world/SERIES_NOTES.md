# Series notes: real world

**Concept:** ancient and mythic figures (Egyptian queens, pharaohs, mummies, workers; Greek gods) living today's modern life — luxury, gadgets, sport, tourism, vlogging — played straight, for comedy or flex.
Built from rw01-rw06 (6 of the 11 reels in this folder). No owner `notes.md` or `prompt.txt` yet; add owner notes here word for word when they come.

## Look
- **Realism:** photoreal, always. Three looks recur: (1) warm golden-hour epic/music-video (rw02, rw05); (2) plain phone or documentary realism with real-looking extras (rw03, rw04, rw06); (3) fisheye selfie vlog (rw01).
- **Palette:** sand, cream and white linen, gold, turquoise/lapis accents; sunset amber; night = indigo sky + orange torchlight. Gods: high-key white marble and gold.
- **Lens:** 24-35 mm for wides, 16 mm or fisheye for selfies, shallow depth of field only on beauty close-ups. All refs are landscape (960-1280 px wide); we always deliver **vertical 9:16**.
- **Grade:** warm, slightly faded film (rw04, rw06) or glossy music-video contrast (rw05). Keep one grade per reel.

## Camera and edit
- Mostly static or slow push-ins; handheld for selfies and reactions; low tracking for vehicles.
- **Average shot length 1.5-2.5 s** (rw06 1.5 s, rw05 1.8 s, rw02 1.9 s, rw01 2.1 s, rw03 2.2 s, rw04 2.5 s). Never longer than ~4 s except an ending push.
- Transitions: hard cuts on the beat. Occasional white flash, dust or sand wipe, or whip past an object.
- Length: 15-73 s; most 15-38 s.

## Hook
- The first 1-2 s already show the gag: an ancient figure + a modern object in one frame (mummy selfie under a paraglider, a god greeting the lens, mummies doing pedicures).
- A close-up "flex" detail by 2 s (gold grill, crown, sneakers).

## Sound
- Music-led (trap/phonk 140 BPM half-time, pop-rap ~95 BPM, afro-house/Arabic pop ~110-120 BPM). rw01 is the exception: deadpan spoken one-liners with captions, one line per shot.
- In clip prompts ask for SFX only, no music; the owner adds the track.

## Recurring characters (ours, original)
- **Queen:** about 25, black bob or braids with gold beads, gold circlet/crown, winged eyeliner, gold collar, white linen. Confident.
- **Mummy king / mummies:** linen bandages, gold jewellery or sunglasses on the king; ordinary-people body language for the extras.
- **Pharaoh:** blue-and-gold striped headdress, white kilt, gold bands. Serious face, silly situation.
- **Roman general:** laurel wreath, white-and-red toga over bronze armour (rw05 romance/rival).
- **Greek gods:** deadpan, sunglasses, modern footwear gag (rw01).

## Do
1. Put one modern object or activity in every shot, and the pyramids/temples in most outdoor shots.
2. Keep costumes historically believable; the comedy comes from the modern element, not silly costumes.
3. Play it straight: serious faces, real physics, real-looking extras who react.
4. Use a payoff gag at the end (sphinx nose, relief pose, prank on a sibling, gem sparkle).
5. Lock cast and style text word for word across all clips.

## Don't
1. No real living people, celebrities or look-alikes; no brand logos or readable text (refs show sportswear logos, a boxed brand name and creator credits — never copy).
2. No guns: swap refs' pistols, miniguns and bazookas for fireworks, fans, confetti, water.
3. No gore or harm to people; slapstick falls only.
4. Don't copy a reel shot with a small swap; change action, prop or setting.
5. Don't mix the three looks inside one reel.

## Added from reels rw07-rw11
