# Space — series notes

**Concept:** a hypnotic, beat-synced montage of extreme cosmic objects (black holes, pulsars, quasars, nebulae) shown huge and close.
**Look (from igexport-DdNDNt0PxMb):** pure black void, one luminous object centred, high contrast, bloom and lens flare;
colour flips between monochrome white, molten orange, electric blue and deep red; occasional digital glitch frame.
**Camera:** mostly locked-off centred framing with slow drift or push; morph/flash transitions instead of hard cuts
(one object turns into the next in a burst of light); one smear/motion-blur whip mid-reel.
**Pacing:** ~22.6 s; a new object or colour state about every 0.5-1 s from 3 s to 18 s; slow open, near-black tail 18-22.6 s.
**Hook:** a white accretion ring flaring out of black in the first second, then a glitch flash at ~2.5 s.
**Sound:** music-driven (peaks hit 0 dB); hits on every flash.
**Do:** one object per beat, centred; strong colour changes on the beat; real-astronomy objects shown dramatically; dark ending.
**Don't:** planets with readable labels, spacecraft logos, people, text, cheesy lens-flare stars on everything.
