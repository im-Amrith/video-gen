# Armor — series notes

**Concept:** a lone armoured warrior commands a force of nature (storm, sea, lightning) in one escalating, mythic moment.
**Look (from igexport-Dc5zCC7IoCI):** monochrome black-and-white, deep crushed blacks, silver highlights, heavy film grain,
photoreal; lightning is the only key light; wet metal specular glints; wide lens on the environment, medium lens on the hero.
**Camera:** one continuous take; slow push-in from an extreme wide toward the hero, then a hero close-up, then a fast
dive/POV into the vortex; ends in a white-out flash. No hard cuts (the source is a single ~14 s generation).
**Pacing:** ~14 s total; slow 0-4 s build, reveal ~4-6 s, escalation 6-10 s, dive + flash 10-14 s.
**Hook:** a tiny silhouette lit by a lightning strike in darkness in the first second.
**Hero:** full plate armour with a horned helm (viking-style), longsword, cape, stands motionless while nature moves.
**Sound:** loud storm bed, thunder cracks, rushing water, sub-boom on the strike; no voice.
**Do:** one hero, nature does the action; lightning as key light; B&W; a continuous camera; end on a flash/white-out.
**Don't:** colour grade, multiple characters, readable text, gore, modern objects, real brand marks.
